Dr. Manal Zamzam
Director, 57357 Healthcare Sciences Academy

Dear Colleagues and Partners, I am pleased to write on behalf of the 57357 Healthcare Sciences Academy — a vision that has become a reality after more than a decade of dedication, perseverance, and collective effort.

This achievement reflects the commitment of everyone who contributed to building this initiative, never sparing effort in turning an idea into a source of lasting impact.

The Academy is more than an educational entity; it is a foundational pillar that supports our people, our profession, and our mission. Our patients have always been our greatest source of motivation, guiding our purpose and shaping a culture that values compassion, excellence, and transformative leadership. This shared commitment has been central to our progress and success.

Throughout this year, we encountered significant challenges that tested us deeply. Yet, these experiences strengthened our capabilities, enhanced our resilience, and contributed to our collective maturity. United by a shared belief in our mission and a clear vision of our goals, the team remained focused and determined until milestones were achieved. Today, we stand proud, contributing to the strength and growth of this remarkable institution, CCHE 57357.

As we reflect on 2025, we are proud to highlight the key achievements and milestones accomplished by the 57357 Healthcare Sciences Academy. These successes would not have been possible without the dedication and belief of those who support our journey.

On behalf of the entire Academy team, I extend my sincere gratitude to our partners, team members, trainers, and supporters. Your trust, collaboration, and commitment continue to make our mission possible and meaningful.

Sincerely.

Recognised at the highest standard.

Two accreditations that confirm the quality of every program we deliver — internationally and nationally.

American Association of Continuing Medical Education®

HSA 57357 has been officially accredited by AACME — recognising that our organization meets the highest international standards for providing quality continuing medical education (CME) programs for physicians and healthcare professionals.

International · CME Provider

Egyptian Health Council (EHC)

Officially accredited as a recognized training center by the Egyptian Health Council. This prestigious accreditation, effective for four years starting May 11, 2025, highlights our commitment to excellence in healthcare education and professional development.

Knowledge into Action · Knowledge into Action

In 2025, the Healthcare Sciences Academy 57357 strategically focused on enhancing the quality of training while responsibly scaling and expanding its educational programs. Throughout the year, the Academy remained committed to growth without compromising educational excellence or training standards.

In response to evolving demands, rapid technological advancements, and a diverse and evolving context for healthcare education, the Academy adopted an agile and adaptive approach to program development and delivery.

Aligned with our motto, “Knowledge into Action,” which emphasizes the integration of theory and practice, the Academy prioritized educational models that bridge academic knowledge with real-world application. This approach has become a core pillar of our identity.

Through continuous evaluation and refinement, the Academy has strengthened its market positioning while maintaining its commitment to excellence, innovation, and learner-centered education.

At the heart of every initiative lies a clear focus on its impact on patient outcomes and the overall patient care journey.
